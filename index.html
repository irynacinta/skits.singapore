Meet The Founder

Iryna Cinta Anuar

Founder, SKits Singapore · Aspiring F1 Engineer

Iryna founded SKits Singapore at just 15 years old. Coming from a regular neighbourhood, she was given the chance to study at a well-known institution in Singapore thanks to financial aid for her studies — an opportunity she has never taken for granted. Now an aspiring Formula 1 engineer, she started this chapter to help pass that same chance on to children growing up in circumstances like her own.

🎯 Fun Facts

  • 🍜🐾 Loves food and animals

  • 🇩🇪 Dreams of studying engineering at a university in Germany

What We Do

STEM opportunities that go beyond the textbook

We connect underprivileged children with tutors, mentors and hands-on experiments — because curiosity deserves more than a worksheet.

📘

Academic Tutoring

One-to-one and small group support in science subjects, matched to each child's level and pace.

🔬

Hands-On Experiments

Beyond the syllabus — building, testing, and blowing things up (safely) to make science stick.

🤝

Community Partnerships

Working alongside Singapore-based humanitarian organisations to reach the children who need it most.

🎒 We support students · Primary 1 – Secondary 4 · Grades 1 – 10

🚧 Heads up: as a brand-new chapter, we don't have activities running just yet — we're currently focused on lining up partner organisations and tutors. Want to help us get there? See below!

Who we're looking for as tutors

We're looking for passionate secondary school students who know their STEM subjects inside and out — well enough to confidently tutor children or run hands-on experiments with them. In return, you'll earn recognised volunteer hours with a credible organisation, and connect with a community of like-minded, STEM-loving students along the way. Interested?
